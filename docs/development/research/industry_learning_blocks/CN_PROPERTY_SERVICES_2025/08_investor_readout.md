# Zhonghai Property industry-context A/B readout

This is an offline product test, not a new investment report or an acceptance
of the existing candidate.

## What changed

The generic input describes one property-services sector and a small panel of
revenue-similar companies. It cannot determine the industry's future path or
which economics Zhonghai is exposed to.

The structured context changes four treatments:

1. The industry path becomes a migration from new-development feeders toward
   stock operations and conditional adjacencies, rather than an unresolved
   generic sector trend.
2. Zhonghai is treated as three different economic paths: a mature residential
   earnings base, public-service growth subject to tenders, renewal and working
   capital, and smart engineering subject to acceptance and cash conversion.
3. Permanent loss is no longer framed mainly as insolvency. The material path
   is years of below-cost reinvestment, delayed collection and inaccessible
   cash while the company remains solvent.
4. Valuation must normalize the three operating paths separately and require
   project maturity, collection and ordinary-shareholder cash evidence. An
   issuer-wide sector multiple or aggregate margin is insufficient.

The mechanism-matched public and institutional reference range narrows from a
non-informative 0%--15% placeholder to 3%--6% ordinary-shareholder operating
margin after working-capital effects. This remains a reference-class scenario;
it is not a Zhonghai project fact and cannot be copied into valuation without
target-company evidence.

## Investor meaning

The result is `MATERIAL_UTILITY`, because the context changes the industry,
exposure, permanent-loss and valuation/evidence treatments and narrows one
like-for-like economic range. It does not grant a buy/sell action, change a
Frozen CJO, or prove that Zhonghai's public projects earn the reference range.

The next live report should verify mature residential margin and collection,
public-project maturity and cumulative cash, smart-engineering contract-asset
conversion, and the accessibility of retained cash. Missing one field narrows
that claim; it does not invalidate the whole industry or company judgment.
