## Finding

cutoff-before official annual-report metadata at CNINFO under a common

independent source-acquisition pass; it is not enough to call any issuer an

on CNINFO during March or April 2018.  A later common 2018 Q3 document can be
added in a proper package if it is relevant, but was intentionally not fetched
here: this reconnaissance only establishes that a primary annual-report route
exists before the cutoff.

## Provisional separation check

The ten codes had no hit in the following current, pre-outcome selection
records: Course 1 frozen worked roster, Course 1 frozen blind-replay roster,
the earlier `CN:000935` cutoff-only candidate ledger, Course 2B / 2C frozen
fairness registers, and the independent AB3 candidate contract.  They also do
not appear in the currently visible cement Pack V3 or the appliance
training-material inventory.

That is deliberately **not** a certificate that the issuers are unseen.  A
real freeze must run one complete identity ledger over every teacher, Pack
path, holdout, target, and campaign selection artifact, including aliases and
control-group conflicts.  This audit does not perform that allocation or
create an exception.

## Acquisition gate before any panel is built

For each eventual candidate, a fresh cutoff-only curator must obtain and
validate at least three consecutive official reporting periods; the issuer
identity and document publication dates; the operating and consolidation
boundary; segment/product carrier evidence; a material cash, working-capital,
or capital-responsibility question; and any relevant 2018 interim disclosure.
Only then may a separate allocator apply the final unseen-company ledger and
freeze a shared-source test cohort.

The ten entries should therefore be treated as a **source-acquisition queue**,
not as a test roster.  They must remain outside both the new Pack-building side
and all learning inputs until the final allocator chooses mutually exclusive
roles.
