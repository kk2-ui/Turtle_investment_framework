## Investor view

The one action worth taking to the next screening gate is a retailer's
already-executed adjustment and upgrade of its direct-store network.  The
investor question is deliberately narrow: did changing the physical retail
network and its assortment improve the customer proposition and store
economics, or would the same path have occurred because of the wider
online/offline expansion?  The source shows that stores were both opened and
replaced or closed before the cutoff; it does **not** answer that question.
