The correct response is not another gate or a stronger mandatory template.

floor, while evaluations distinguish:

- defensive refusal, where the agent uses uncertainty to avoid a current
  judgment;

Future review should call out defensive writing when it actually removes a
best-current judgment, economic mechanism, investment consequence, or
reversal condition. It should not fail sound work merely because a preferred
heading or field is absent.
