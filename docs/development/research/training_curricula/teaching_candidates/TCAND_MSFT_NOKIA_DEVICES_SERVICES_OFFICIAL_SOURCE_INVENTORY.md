## Source-use guardrails

- The $7.1bn cash component is capital deployment; the $7.5bn impairment is a
  later carrying-value write-down. Do not sum them into a claimed cash loss.
- The official release's forecast language (faster innovation, synergies,
  branding) is a record of the decision rationale only. It is not evidence
  that the forecast was realized.
- The FY2015 filing supports a Phone Hardware episode, not an inference about
  the value of every retained Nokia patent/license, Microsoft's total mobile
  strategy, or a counterfactual alliance outcome.
- Mechanism identity for curriculum indexing:
  `PARTNER_TO_OWNER_HARDWARE_ECOSYSTEM -> INTEGRATION_EXECUTION -> CUSTOMER_VOLUME_AND_MARGIN_MIX -> FUTURE_CASH_FLOW_RESET -> IMPAIRMENT_AND_RESTRUCTURING`.
