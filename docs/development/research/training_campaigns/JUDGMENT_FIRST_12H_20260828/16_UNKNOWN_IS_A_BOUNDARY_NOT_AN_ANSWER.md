# UNKNOWN Is a Boundary, Not an Answer

## Decision

`UNKNOWN` and `PUBLIC_INFO_UNAVAILABLE` remain legitimate evidence states, but
they no longer complete a research question or a reader-facing company topic
by themselves.

The required output is the best bounded judgment that remains after the
unknown is localized: what claim is affected, how the base case changes, what
economic consequence is retained, and which observable result would support
or reverse that treatment.

## Observed failure

Two active paths still rewarded abstention:

- a targeted research task could finish with “public information unavailable,
  keep everything unknown and unchanged”;
- a report made of seven UNKNOWN paragraphs could close every reader topic,
  even when only one paragraph had a source and none contained an enterprise
  judgment.

The first attempted correction merely turned the same abstention into five
text fields. The second attempted correction accepted a list of closure words.
Independent counterexamples showed that both could still be completed by
form-filling rather than judgment. Those versions were not accepted.

Root cause: `MODEL + REASONING + WRITING`.

Economic impact: research effort could stop exactly where investor judgment
was most needed. Missing customer, cash-access, or governance evidence could
erase the remaining operating conclusion, while the report appeared complete
and auditable.

## Runtime correction

### Targeted research

An unresolved or unavailable finding now binds to the exact frozen research
question and states:

- the affected economic axis;
- whether the prior claim is conditionally retained, narrowed, excluded from
  the base case, reversed, or retained only as a range;
- the actual base-case treatment and economic consequence;
- one specific metric or event, plus different support and reversal branches
  expressed either as a numeric operator/value/unit or as a named event that
  occurs or does not occur.

“Future disclosure,” “an operating indicator,” or “if it supports the judgment
then retain it” is not a discriminating observation. No decision change is
required, but a current economic position is. Event definitions cannot refer
back to “supporting” or “reversing” the judgment; the observation itself must
be independently settleable.

### Reader coverage

UNKNOWN-only prose cannot substitute for the business mechanism, normal-
earnings route, ordinary-share cash access, valuation identity, permanent-loss
case, or monitoring logic. A mixed paragraph may still contain a valid
affirmative mechanism and a local unknown: the affirmative clause is evaluated
without letting the unknown erase it.

The data-boundary topic itself requires a local source, a specific economic
object, an actual base-case treatment, a specific observation, and both support
and reversal consequences. A wall of closure keywords does not pass.

## Boundaries retained

- Lack of public evidence remains a valid stopping condition after the bounded
  source plan has genuinely been attempted.
- UNKNOWN does not become negative evidence.
- A local unknown does not force a company-wide downgrade or a chapter rewrite.
- The system does not demand fabricated precision, probabilities, thresholds,
  or facts.
- Material unsupported claims, invented source identities, PIT violations, and
  outcome leakage remain blocked by their existing contracts.

## Mechanical acceptance

- bare unavailable finding: incomplete;
- defensive form-filled closure: invalid;
- exact claim binding plus owner-cash base-case exclusion and two observable
  branches: valid;
- seven-paragraph UNKNOWN wall: blocked;
- seven-paragraph closure-keyword wall: blocked;
- affirmative customer/pricing mechanism followed by a local retention unknown
  in the same paragraph: accepted as a business judgment;
- localized cash boundary with base treatment and two-way observation: accepted.

This is not a new evidence threshold. It removes the reward for making no
judgment when evidence is imperfect.
