# Local Axes and One Canonical Company Judgment

## Decision

A supported operating mechanism can remain the current primary explanation
when owner cash or permanent-loss transmission is locally unknown. That local
uncertainty closes only the quantitative identity, BuyBand, or action that
depends on it.

When a report is bound to an independently frozen CJO, the reader summary now
comes from that CJO. A stale report-local thesis is not a second truth source.

## Observed failure

The previous runtime could collapse:

```text
operating mechanism improves
owner cash unknown
permanent loss unknown
```

into a company-wide `MIXED` or `NO_PRIMARY`. Downstream admission then required
every judgment, variable, and transmission to be bound even when the consumer
used only the operating path.

The first correction preserved the operating path but exposed another material
error: an overlay request could claim `D4=CLOSED` and permanent loss `LOW`,
overriding a Frozen CJO whose cash or loss axis was unknown, ineligible, or
deteriorating. That could open a non-null conditional buy boundary.

A separate reader-path error allowed assembly to validate a canonical Frozen
CJO read receipt, then render its summary from `thesis_test.json` instead.

Root cause: `MODEL + REASONING + WRITING`.

Economic impact: the first error discarded useful normal-earnings judgment;
the second could turn the restored operating judgment into unjustified price
or action authority; the third could show investors a stale or more defensive
company conclusion than the one independently reviewed.

## Runtime correction

- CJO `resolution` selects the evidenced central operating mechanism. Normal
  earnings, owner cash, and permanent loss remain separate transmission axes.
- current-company admission validates the judgments, variables, transmissions,
  cash normalization, and allocation events actually consumed downstream.
- a normal-earnings value identity and expectation-gap reference may remain as
  research-only when the cash or loss axis is open;
- effective quantitative closure is the conservative intersection of the
  Frozen CJO axis, the admission binding actually consumed, and the overlay
  request;
- `UNKNOWN`, `MODEL_UNCERTAIN`, `EVIDENCE_INELIGIBLE`, or `DETERIORATES` in cash
  or permanent loss cannot be overwritten by an optimistic request;
- owner-cash capitalization and every cash-dependent BuyBand/action remain
  closed until both the CJO and the consumed binding support them;
- report assembly rebuilds the bound `JUDGMENT_SYNTHESIS` projection and renders
  the Frozen CJO directly. Legacy local-thesis rendering is used only when no
  canonical CJO is bound;
- a no-primary Frozen CJO still displays supported company judgments, strongest
  counterargument, local cash/loss boundaries, and reversal observations.

## Boundaries retained

- Operating PRIMARY is not an investment recommendation.
- A research-only normal-earnings range does not become owner cash or a
  conditional buy price.
- High or unknown permanent-loss exposure keeps the BuyBand closed.
- The Frozen CJO remains immutable, independently reviewed, PIT-bound, and
  without valuation, price, report-release, or investment authority.
- A central path that itself consumes ineligible evidence is still rejected;
  only an unrelated ineligible downstream trace is localized.

## Mechanical acceptance

- supported operating trace plus ineligible cash trace outside the central
  path: operating PRIMARY retained, cash axis unknown;
- operating-only admission: accepted without pretending cash closure;
- unknown or deteriorating cash/loss plus optimistic request: normal-earnings
  research reference retained, owner-cash identity unavailable, BuyBand null;
- fully supported cash/loss CJO plus consumed bindings: conditional candidate
  band remains available, still without trading authority;
- canonical Frozen CJO and stale local thesis conflict: reader summary follows
  the Frozen CJO;
- no-primary Frozen CJO: local company judgments remain visible.

The correction preserves more enterprise judgment and grants less unsupported
action authority at the same time. That is judgment-first behavior, not a new
global gate.
