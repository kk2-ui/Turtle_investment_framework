# Chapter Depth Is Not Judgment Quality

## Decision

The active report pipeline no longer treats chapter length, number count,
formula count, heading count, available-file volume, or raw citation count as
evidence of judgment quality. The chapter-depth contract now has one job:
reject an empty body.

This is a reward-function correction, not a relaxation of PIT, evidence,
model, valuation, or decision discipline.

## Observed failure

The prior unified path turned six regular-expression counts into publication
gates and repair targets. Data-rich runs automatically raised those quotas.
An otherwise usable reference chapter failed because it had eleven raw source
anchors rather than twelve; appending a duplicate source anchor changed it to
PASS without changing any fact or judgment.

Root cause: `MODEL + WRITING`.

Economic impact: the cheapest strategy was to add numbers, repeat sources,
split headings, lengthen prose, or retain defensive UNKNOWN language. Paid
repair cycles optimized form while the current company judgment, strongest
rival explanation, owner-cash implication, and permanent-loss risk could stay
unchanged.

## Runtime correction

- `chapter_depth` retains metrics as diagnostics, but only an empty substantive
  body can fail the depth status.
- `data_rich` and `source_deepening` no longer raise writing or citation quotas.
- source deepening continues to enforce the actual source-research plan.
- the idempotent chapter path no longer requires an existing chapter to have
  at least 800 characters before it can be reused.
- a repeated raw source anchor cannot unlock completion.
- the generation prompt asks for the current judgment, economic mechanism,
  strongest rival, scenarios, reversal facts, and investment implication. It
  explicitly prohibits padding with numbers, formulas, headings, or sources.

## Boundaries retained

The following remain independent blockers when their own contracts apply:

- missing chapter file or chapter identity;
- empty body or explicit placeholder;
- unsupported material claim under claim-evidence validation;
- invalid model identity or valuation derivation;
- contradiction with canonical decision values or permissions;
- PIT, outcome isolation, responsibility boundary, and custodian violations.

An unavailable immaterial field remains local. It does not require a longer
chapter and cannot cancel the rest of the company judgment.

## Mechanical acceptance

- concise material judgment without numeric padding: PASS;
- data-rich and standard depth requirements: identical;
- standard and source-deepening depth requirements: identical;
- same chapter with one versus twenty duplicate anchors: same completion
  status;
- unchanged Gree reference Ch4: PASS without adding its twelfth anchor;
- headings and source annotation with no substantive body: FAIL;
- targeted regression set: `118 passed`.

This change does not claim that training effectiveness is proven. It removes
one incentive that made defensive, repetitive writing the rational strategy.
