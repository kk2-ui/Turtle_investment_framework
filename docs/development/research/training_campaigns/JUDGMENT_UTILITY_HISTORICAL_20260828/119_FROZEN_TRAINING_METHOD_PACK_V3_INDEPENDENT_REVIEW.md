# Independent review — Method Pack V3

**Verdict: ACCEPT.** V3 is an evidence-bound, non-defensive carry-forward candidate. It makes no validation or investment-authority claim and is fit only for the specified next paired historical Holdout evaluation.

## Review boundary

Read only `AGENTS.md`, V2, V3, and the six expressly permitted Blind-feedback receipts 47, 71, 83, 94, 107, and 117. No Teaching, Holdout, prospective, non-listed case, price, return, valuation, or report material was used. This review does not assess any company outcome or whether the candidate method is useful; it assesses the pack's contract and provenance.

## Evidence and reasoning

1. **Carry-forward is exact and evidence-bound.** After removing V3-only provenance fields (`v3_vs_v2_disposition` and `direct_source_ref`), its three rule objects are byte-for-byte identical to V2; no rule, trigger, threshold, prohibited inference, or required behavior was changed. Permissions are likewise identical to V2 and all are `NONE`.

   The no-new-rule conclusion is supported rather than used as a defensive refusal. Receipts 71 and 83 each say the frozen contract already supplied the relevant split and local treatment, with no next-case field, threshold, or research-action delta. Receipt 94 records no frozen field/processing delta; receipt 107 records a material company-treatment update but no field, threshold, or processing change for another unknown company; and receipt 117 expressly denies creation of a transferable rule. Receipt 47 is correctly treated as V2 provenance, not post-V2 utility credit. Thus the result is: retain useful company feedback and the existing candidate behaviors, while not claiming a new transfer rule from these episodes.

2. **No next-Holdout result leakage or result-specific method parameterization.** V3 names only the permitted, already-closed feedback receipts as provenance. Its five ledger entries summarize why those prior receipts did not change the generic rules; they do not alter a trigger, add a threshold, encode an expected result, identify the next paired Holdout, or provide outcome content for that Holdout. The closed-receipt summaries are evidence for a non-change conclusion, not a result-conditioned instruction. No price, return, valuation, report, or investment-action field is present.

3. **No validation or permission claim.** `purpose`, `allowed_utility_standard`, `claims_explicitly_not_made`, and `permissions` consistently keep method validation, transfer validation, CJO, formal valuation, buy band, report, and investment action at `NONE`/`false`. A completed future pair could only support the contract's stated preference test; it cannot retroactively turn this release into validation by assertion.

4. **The paired-Holdout test is fair and non-defensive.** Before outcome access it requires a *specific*, material Baseline-versus-Enhanced economic-treatment difference, including mechanism and expected decision consequence. Both arms must share the cutoff-bounded result-unseen evidence, tools, and research time/turn budget. Differences that are merely prose, fields, lower `UNKNOWN`, or additional/stricter gates are expressly not evaluable. These are evaluation-integrity conditions, not a new research-admission or evidence-volume gate. If the pre-frozen arm difference is absent or the arms are unequal, the defined result is `NOT_EVALUABLE`, not a favorable method conclusion.

5. **The rules remain investor-useful.** The first rule confines a withheld upgrade to the unmatched economic bridge and preserves independently supported judgments. The second confines a conversion pause to the incremental burden and retains established enterprise strengths. The third requires responsibility-matched subaxis treatment, permits an enterprise downgrade only with a material carrier or demonstrated transmission, and retires settled questions from the next rank-one action. Each localizes uncertainty and directs a decision-relevant treatment or next observation; none permits an `UNKNOWN` whole-company stop.

## Decision path

`ACCEPT` → keep V3 frozen as `RESEARCH_METHOD_ONLY` candidate → before any paired-Holdout outcome access, freeze the material Baseline/Enhanced economic treatment difference under the same cutoff, evidence, tools, and budget → assess only the stated material judgment/treatment effect.

Any pair that lacks that pre-frozen material difference or equal arms must be recorded as `NOT_EVALUABLE`; it cannot support method or transfer validation. No repository files were modified.
