# CN300059 V4 Independent Pre-Outcome Pair Review

## Verdict

`RETURN_PAIR_NOT_EVALUABLE`

FY2024 outcome access is **not authorized**. FY2024 remains sealed, and neither frozen arm may be revised.

## Evidence from the completed blind pair

The resource comparison passes. Receipt 140 releases the same three FY2021-FY2023 reports and local text extractions, with local search/text extraction only and a three-report-reading-plus-one-synthesis-turn allocation for each arm. Receipt 141 records that Baseline used that exact 3+1 allocation and no outcome or external material; receipt 143 records the same for Enhanced.

The required actual, material treatment difference does not pass. Baseline's completed `material_activity_customer_assets_carrier.perimeter_boundary` already says that TTFund activity is on the TTFund-platform perimeter, while the related income is on the financial-electronic-commerce reportable-segment perimeter; it explicitly says they must not be combined into monetisation, margin, or cash-conversion claims. Its frozen treatment consequently retains only a demand signal and withholds a carrier-to-economics bridge, with a cycle-sensitive normal-earnings treatment and no owner-cash conversion credit.

Enhanced reaches the same operative result under `COMPOSITE_METRIC_SCOPE_BEFORE_CONVERSION_CLAIM`: it withholds fee-conversion, recurring-profit, owner-cash, capital-efficiency, and capital-light valuation credit for TTFund activity because no identical perimeter or issuer bridge is disclosed. Both arms therefore keep the same economic mechanism in the base case: platform activity is informative about reach/demand but cannot support a TTFund activity-to-fee/profit/cash premium; normal earnings and valuation direction remain restrained, and owner cash remains uncredited.

Enhanced labels and documents this scope discipline more explicitly, and the two arms phrase follow-up cells differently. Neither difference changes the completed normal-earnings, owner-cash, permanent-loss, valuation-direction, or rank-one research treatment. The V4 pack's statement that the Enhanced treatment is *capable* of differing from an alternative is not evidence that this completed Baseline actually differed. Accordingly, a FY2024 result could not adjudicate incremental V4 utility: it would observe two economically equivalent pre-outcome treatments.

## Return basis and remediation

- Root cause: `MODEL` — the paired evaluation did not create the required realized baseline-versus-V4 treatment contrast. This is not a data-coverage failure: both completed arms had the same adequate cutoff-bounded packet for the comparison they were asked to make.
- Economic impact: outcome agreement would not show that V4 avoided a normal-earnings, owner-cash, permanent-loss, valuation-direction, or research-priority error. It could only show that two arms which already made the same scoped treatment were jointly supported or unsupported.
- Missing comparative fact: an actual, completed Baseline treatment that materially differs from Enhanced on whether and how the TTFund activity carrier receives economic conversion credit, with a stated consequence for normal earnings, owner cash, valuation direction, permanent-loss treatment, or rank-one research action.
- Prohibited assumptions: do not assume incremental V4 utility from its label, extra perimeter prose, additional cells, caveats, `UNKNOWN`s, or a hypothetical baseline that grants a conversion premium. Do not treat a later outcome as discriminating when the frozen treatments are the same.
- Executable remediation: leave 142 and 144 unchanged and keep FY2024 sealed. Run a fresh, independently selected and outcome-sealed paired holdout under the same source/tool/3+1 parity. Before any outcome access, independently freeze the two completed arms and review whether a material economic treatment difference actually arose from the active V4 rule; do not force a weaker Baseline or manufacture a difference through added fields, prose, caveats, `UNKNOWN`s, or gates. If the arms again converge economically, return that pair without accessing its outcome.
- Acceptance criteria for outcome authorization on a fresh pair: (1) completed arm receipts prove identical cutoff packet, tools, and 3+1 allocation; (2) the actual frozen outputs contain a V4-attributable difference in normal earnings, owner cash, permanent loss, valuation direction, or rank-one research treatment; (3) the economic mechanism and pre-frozen outcome-resolution cells can test that difference; and (4) both arms are irrevocably frozen before FY2024 is opened. Only then may the specified post-cutoff cells be accessed for adjudication.
