## Investor interpretation

Both product buckets sold more volume—茅台酒46,412.95吨 and other series 36,919.81吨—so the result supports the cutoff view that product absorption and customer payment had not broadly broken. Goods collections rose to 1,826.45亿元 and reported operating cash to 924.64亿元.

The channel conclusion is nevertheless locally mixed: i茅台 sales fell to 200.24亿元 from the frozen 223.74亿元 base. Direct/wholesale gains cannot turn that leg into success; conversely, that leg cannot erase broader product-volume and goods-collection evidence. Comprehensive operating cash is partly affected by finance-company pooling, so it is not wholly incremental product cash conversion.

The strongest remaining countercase is channel redistribution, price/mix or inventory movement concealed by direct/wholesale growth, with i茅台 as a channel-friction signal. The conclusion becomes materially weaker if comparable future disclosure couples direct/i茅台 expansion with wholesale or product-volume decline, both product buckets fail to exceed their FY2023 bases, or goods collections and operating cash both weaken without a sufficient non-product explanation. No valuation, return or investment conclusion is authorized.
