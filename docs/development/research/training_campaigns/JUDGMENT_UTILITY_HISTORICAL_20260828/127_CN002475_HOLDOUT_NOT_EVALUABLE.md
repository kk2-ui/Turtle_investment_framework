## What is retained

This is not defensive writing and it is not a company-level refusal. The usable pre-outcome company judgment remains intact: Luxshare is a scaled, capable precision-manufacturing operator; its FY2023 delivery, inventory absorption, and reported cash performance deserve positive operating credit; its owner-cash run rate remains unproven because the cash increase was working-capital assisted; and customer/program concentration, the large manufacturing asset base, and utilization remain an open permanent-loss carrier rather than evidence of present enterprise failure.

Those are the actual treatments in both arms. Each credits operating capability without calling the recovery durable, declines to capitalize the FY2023 cash peak as recurring owner cash, and preserves concentration/asset-utilization risk without withdrawing independently supported execution credit. The baseline's conservative owner-cash range and the enhanced arm's `CONDITIONAL` wording are not a material semantic difference. This disposition neither changes that company view nor invents a baseline weakness in order to credit V3.

## Disposition basis

The frozen pack requires a specific, material Baseline-versus-Enhanced economic-treatment difference before outcome access. The common cutoff and three-document packet were preserved, and neither arm accessed outcomes. But the enhanced arm's stated contrast was with a counterfactual ordinary-research treatment that would capitalize FY2023 cash and reduce permanent-loss concern. That is not the actual baseline: the baseline already made the same cash-normalization, core-margin, and concentration-risk treatment.

Additional segmentation of outcome cells, more explicit labels, or different prose can improve diagnostic description, but does not establish a V3-driven difference in normal earnings, owner cash, permanent loss, valuation direction, or the rank-one research action. They cannot be treated as method utility.

## Required boundary for a future pair

The only allowed next action is to choose a new, independently firewalled paired Holdout **only after** a genuine V3-driven semantic difference exists between the actual baseline and enhanced pre-outcome treatments. Before either arm receives outcome material, the new pair must record:

1. The exact economic mechanism and treatment difference—normal earnings, owner cash, permanent loss, valuation direction, or a rank-one research action—that V3 changes.
2. Identical cutoff-bounded documents, a common permitted-tool allowance, and the same concrete time/turn budget for both arms.
3. The frozen observations that could show the enhanced treatment improved, worsened, or did not improve the stated company judgment.
4. An independent result-free declaration that the completed actual arm outputs contain that material V3-attributable difference.

Only then may that new pair advance under its separately controlled outcome-resolution process. This Luxshare FY2024 outcome remains unavailable to it.
