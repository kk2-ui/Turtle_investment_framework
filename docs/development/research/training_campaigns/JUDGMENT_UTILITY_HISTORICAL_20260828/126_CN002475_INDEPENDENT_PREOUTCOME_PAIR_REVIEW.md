## Return contract

**Root-cause classification:** `REASONING` and `ACQUISITION_MODULE`.

- `REASONING`: the enhanced arm's declared ordinary-research contrast was framed against an assumed counterfactual rather than the actual baseline. The baseline independently applied the same core caution to cash normalization, margin inference, and concentration/asset-utilization risk.
- `ACQUISITION_MODULE`: the common packet records equal documents and time boundary, but not a numerical common time/turn or tool allocation. There is no evidence this created an arm difference; the missing record prevents a stronger allocation-equality claim.

**Economic impact:** No outcome result can be attributed to V3 rather than to ordinary competent pre-outcome research. Unsealing FY2024 would create a false method-utility claim from two arms that already make the same investor-relevant treatment. It does not change the pre-outcome company view: Luxshare remains a capable but concentrated, capital-sensitive operator whose FY2023 cash peak is not a demonstrated normal owner-cash level.

**Missing facts:** A material, actual arm-to-arm pre-outcome treatment difference attributable to V3; and, for a future fully auditable pair, an explicitly frozen common tool and time/turn allowance. Outcome data are not a missing fact to cure this return and must remain unread.

**Prohibited assumptions:** Do not assume the baseline would capitalize the FY2023 cash peak; do not infer a V3 effect from enhanced-only wording, a `CONDITIONAL` label, a lower/higher field count, or more granular frozen cells; do not use FY2024 results to manufacture a pre-outcome difference.

**Executable remediation:** Preserve this case as non-evaluable and retain the seal. For a new independently firewalled holdout, freeze before outcome access (1) the exact actual-arm semantic contrast, expressed as different treatment of owner cash, normal earnings, permanent loss, or a rank-one research decision; and (2) common documents, tools, and a concrete time/turn allocation. Have an independent reviewer compare the completed actual arm outputs before outcome access.

**Acceptance criteria for a future evaluable pair:**

1. Both arms use the identical cutoff, source packet, tools, and recorded budget.
2. The completed baseline and enhanced outputs contain at least one specific, non-cosmetic economic treatment difference attributable to the released V3 rule.
3. The difference specifies its mechanism, the required frozen cells, and a fair post-outcome criterion that could show the enhanced treatment improved, worsened, or did not improve the company judgment.
4. The independent reviewer declares that difference before any outcome material is accessed.
