# CN601012 Blind 11 — Independent Pre-Outcome Review

**Verdict: RETURN**

## Boundary and independent assessment

This review used only the selection register (158), packet receipt (159),
forecaster receipt (160), frozen judgment (161), and the three released
FY2021–FY2023 annual-report text extractions named in 159. No FY2024-or-later
material, campaign/curriculum/method artifact, external/browser source, or
market, return, valuation, or analyst material was read.

The enterprise judgment itself is packet-derived, investor-useful, and not a
defensive abstention. The FY2023 report supports the central distinction:
reported wafer shipments reached 125.42GW, external cell sales 5.90GW, and
module shipments 67.52GW, while prices collapsed, inventory and fixed-asset
provisions reached RMB6.757bn, operating cash flow was RMB8.117bn, and cash
capital expenditure was RMB9.256bn. The same admissible report supports the
counterweight of RMB57.001bn reported cash. Thus the forecaster properly treats
scale and liquidity as mitigating immediate failure risk without treating them
as proof of normal owner cash or recoverable value of all new capacity.

That is the right economic framing: a capital-intensive manufacturer can remain
liquid and strategically relevant while a price war and repeated technology
refresh destroy returns on inventory and long-lived assets. The stated base
case, countercases, local unknowns, and directional valuation implications are
appropriately conditional and do not use price or outcome information.

## Material return: the frozen resolution contract is incomplete

**Root cause: REASONING.** The defect is not missing historical packet support
for the judgment. It is that three of the four purported FY2024 mechanical
cells leave a decision-relevant measurement or result treatment to the outcome
operator. That would permit post-reveal discretion over the capacity/volume,
margin, and permanent-loss branches.

| Cell | Completeness result | Material defect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| FY2024_CELL_1 | Return | The target is `电池加组件出货量` of 90–100GW, whereas the frozen FY2023 comparator is an arithmetic combination of 67.52GW module shipment and 5.90GW *external* cell sales. Judgment 161 expressly says that combination is not perfectly like-for-like with the earlier target, which included self-use. The cell never fixes whether FY2024 cells used internally, module self-use, or only external cell sales are included, nor the treatment if the annual report does not disclose the exact aggregate. |
| FY2024_CELL_2 | Return | The product-table scope is identifiable (`组件及电池`; FY2023 margin 18.38%), and 18.38% is a threshold. But the test also requires “revenue direction” and the upward condition requires higher revenue, while no resolution is frozen for a mixed result: margin at/above 18.38% with flat/lower revenue. That result can matter to whether a spread is durable, yet is neither upward, downward, nor explicitly retained at the base judgment. |
| FY2024_CELL_3 | Pass | Consolidated operating cash flow less cash paid for fixed, intangible, and other long-lived assets is a defined screen, with a non-negative threshold and a limited owner-cash/permanent-loss use. It is correctly not represented as precise free cash flow. |
| FY2024_CELL_4 | Return | “Debt/cash buffer weakened” and “material additional impairment” have no formula, comparison baseline/threshold, or joint-result rule. The listed obligations do not specify how current portions are disaggregated or avoid double counting, and “material” is not quantified or otherwise fixed before reveal. Consequently the outcome operator could decide after seeing FY2024 whether a change in cash, financing, or impairment is adverse enough to move J3. |

The FY2023 source itself demonstrates why CELL_1 and CELL_4 cannot be left
implicit. The FY2022 plan described its 85GW battery-and-module target as
including self-use; the FY2023 report separately disclosed external cell sales
and module shipments. Its balance sheet also has distinct lines for monetary
funds, short-term borrowings, current non-current liabilities, long-term
borrowings, bonds, and lease liabilities. Those are not interchangeable
measurement bases.

### Economic impact

This can materially change the investment conclusion. A near-threshold FY2024
shipment result could be called a demand-realisation success or a miss depending
on the selected self-use/cell convention. More importantly, an unspecified cash
buffer and impairment rule can turn the same FY2024 balance-sheet facts into
either “liquidity buffer intact” or “permanent-loss branch strengthening.” That
would change the normal-owner-cash range, the recoverable-value treatment of the
asset base, and the directional valuation range—the central purpose of the
episode.

### Missing facts and prohibited assumptions

The missing facts to be obtained only after a repaired judgment is re-frozen are
the FY2024 actual shipment components, the same-scope product-table revenue and
margin, consolidated operating cash and cash capex, and the specified
cash/financing/impairment fields. The missing item *now* is the frozen
measurement contract that says exactly how those facts will be used.

The outcome operator must not assume that any separately reported cell and
module series is comparable to the 90GW threshold, infer an unstated
self-use convention, net all reported cash against whichever liabilities make
the result attractive, treat any impairment as “material,” or select an
impairment/buffer threshold after seeing FY2024. Nor may it convert the cash
capex screen into precise free cash flow or an investment action.

### Executable remediation

Before any FY2024 acquisition or outcome review, the forecaster must revise and
re-freeze the resolution section using the same FY2021–FY2023 packet only:

1. For CELL_1, specify the exact FY2024 disclosure label(s), inclusion/exclusion
   of externally sold and self-used cells and modules, aggregation arithmetic,
   and a no-proxy treatment if that exact series is unavailable. Keep the 90GW
   threshold and state the resulting J1 treatment for at-least-90GW, below-90GW,
   and unavailable/non-comparable results.
2. For CELL_2, keep the consolidated product-table category and 18.38% margin
   comparison, explicitly set FY2023 product revenue as the revenue base, and
   map every margin/revenue quadrant—including margin at/above 18.38% on
   non-expanding revenue—to upward, downward, or unchanged J2 treatment.
3. For CELL_4, define one reproducible consolidated cash-buffer formula, its
   component balance-sheet/notes lines, the FY2023 comparison baseline, a
   precommitted weakening threshold, and a precommitted inventory-plus-fixed-
   asset impairment materiality threshold. Define how every combination of the
   CELL_3 screen, buffer result, and impairment result maps to upward,
   unchanged, or downward J3 treatment. The threshold may use an explicitly
   stated FY2023 packet anchor, but may not be selected from FY2024 facts.
4. Reissue the execution receipt to attest that the repaired forecaster text was
   frozen before any outcome material was acquired. No change to the underlying
   evidence boundary, market-price boundary, or enterprise thesis is required.

## Acceptance criteria

Accept on resubmission only if each of the four cells has a fixed annual-report
scope, field definition and arithmetic, comparison baseline, threshold, and
exhaustive outcome-to-judgment resolution; a future operator can apply those
instructions without judgmental metric selection; and the reissued forecaster
receipt attests that the repaired text is frozen before reveal. The company
judgment may remain conditional, but the update rules may not.

This return does not authorize outcome acquisition. The current forecaster
artifact remains a pre-outcome artifact, but it is not accepted for reveal until
the above pre-reveal repair is independently cleared.
