view. FY2023 records 125.42GW wafer shipments, 5.90GW external cell sales, and
67.52GW component shipments, while the same report describes a supply-demand
imbalance, prices below RMB1/W for components and approximately 70%/60%/50%

supports the central distinction between shipment scale and durable economics.

appropriately about capital absorption, technology replacement and impaired
