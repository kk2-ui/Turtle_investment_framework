# 康佳集团股份有限公司：企业承保读本

The pre-cutoff record supports a conditional, not base, recurring-enterprise assessment: adjusted earnings stayed negative despite reported FY2017 profit, while cash conversion and funding pressure are unresolved at the component level.

观察时域：FY2015-FY2017 pre-cutoff normalization window

最可能的行业路径：A mixed enterprise whose consolidated reported earnings are not a reliable recurring operating indicator because FY2017 includes a stated non-sustainable disposal gain.

利润池传导：Revenue growth and supply-chain-management sales can enter enterprise economics through product mix, trading volume, margin, receivables, inventories, and funding needs, but the source does not supply a component-level bridge from those variables to earnings or cash.

公司暴露：The group includes multimedia television/display sales, white goods, mobile phones, and supply-chain management trading, with nationwide and overseas multimedia channels and material receivable and inventory balances at the consolidated level.

适应能力：In FY2017 the company integrated multimedia around television/display and began company-form operation for multimedia sales, but the evidence does not quantify the resulting unit economics or cash conversion.

正常经济与普通股现金：Consolidated adjusted net profit improved from large losses but remained negative across FY2015-FY2017; a normalized recurring range cannot be bounded from the supplied consolidated disclosures.

永久损失路径：Negative FY2017 operating cash flow linked to receivable and unexpired-bill increases, rising receivables and inventories, and sizeable short-term debt create a financing and cash-conversion loss path that cannot be allocated by business component.

价值处理：Only a conditional recurring-enterprise route is retained; disposal gains and project-capital effects are excluded from the base and working-capital/financing effects remain unresolved.

## 行业处境、公司位置与适应

FY2017 revenue expanded sharply, but the reported profit was dominated by a stated non-sustainable property-disposal gain while the consolidated adjusted-profit series remained negative in FY2015, FY2016, and FY2017. The permitted record identifies several operating businesses but does not allocate operating cash, capital spending, working capital, or borrowings to them.

The enterprise has multiple disclosed operating components and channels, but the permitted disclosures do not establish a component-level margin, customer concentration, cash-conversion, or capital-allocation position.

The FY2017 multimedia integration and new operating-company arrangement are observable organizational changes, not evidence of a realized recurring-margin or owner-cash improvement.

Use the consolidated adjusted-net-profit history as a conditional reference only: it was negative in all three supplied years, while FY2017 reported profit is distorted by a disclosed non-sustainable disposal gain. No component-level normalized earnings bridge is available.

Keep normal earnings conditional and unquantified; do not treat the FY2017 disposal-driven reported profit as recurring.

Do not form a base owner-cash range because operating cash, working capital, long-lived-asset spending, and borrowing are only consolidated and FY2017 operating cash was negative.
