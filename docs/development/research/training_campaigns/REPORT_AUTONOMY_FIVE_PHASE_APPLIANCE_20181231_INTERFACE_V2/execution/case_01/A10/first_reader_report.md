# 康佳集团股份有限公司：企业承保读本

The reported revenue expansion and narrowing adjusted loss do not establish recurring owner economics because component earnings and cash are not disclosed, while FY2017 consolidated cash conversion weakened alongside larger working-capital balances and short-term debt.

## 行业未来与公司传导

观察时域：FY2018 onward from the 2017 annual-report information set

最可能的行业路径：The supplied record establishes product and channel exposure but does not establish a durable industry profit-pool regime or a company-specific advantage.

利润池传导：Multimedia B2B/B2C and supply-chain trading can transmit growth into receivables, inventory, and financing needs; the supplied disclosures do not allocate margins, cash conversion, or capital returns by component.

公司暴露：The group is exposed through domestic and export television/display activity, internet activity, supply-chain trade, white goods, and mobile phones, with project reserves outside the disclosed operating mix.

适应能力：Management integrated multimedia around television and display products and began a company-based operating arrangement; this is an organizational adaptation, not evidence of a proven change in responsibility-matched economics.

正常经济与普通股现金：Consolidated adjusted attributable losses narrowed from FY2015 to FY2017, but remained negative in FY2017 and cannot be separated into component-normal earnings from this package.

永久损失路径：The central durable-loss path is continued cash absorption through receivables, inventory, debt rollover, and unproven project retention while recurring operating economics remain unallocated; the disposal gain does not resolve that path.

价值处理：Use only a conditional continuing-owner-cash route; exclude the disposal as recurring economics and leave growth-return treatment unresolved until matched component evidence exists.

最强竞争解释：No named rival is supplied; the relevant strongest rival is the unidentified television/display and supply-chain competitor set whose relative unit economics are not disclosed.

## 行业处境、公司位置与适应

FY2017 revenue expanded and the adjusted attributable loss narrowed, but the package does not provide component earnings or cash attribution; the reported cash conversion weakened while receivables, inventory, and short-term debt increased.

The disclosed business perimeter is diversified across multimedia, supply-chain management, white goods, and mobile phones. The evidence supports the business map and 2017 multimedia reorganization, but not a component-level margin, customer concentration, or cash-position comparison.

The television/display-oriented multimedia integration and the new sales entity are a live adaptation whose economic effect remains conditional because subsequent component earnings, cash, and capital-return evidence are absent.

## 生存、正常盈利与普通股现金

FY2017 short-term borrowings were RMB 6.927 billion and long-term borrowings RMB 167 million, with RMB 9.998 billion borrowings received and RMB 9.541 billion debt repayments in consolidated financing cash flow. This demonstrates refinancing activity and working-capital pressure, not a component-level solvency conclusion.

The FY2017 attributable result included RMB 6.5878 billion investment income from the disposal of 70% of 康侨佳城, labelled non-sustainable in the annual report. It is subtracted as a disclosed nonrecurring item and is not treated as recurring earnings.

Use the consolidated adjusted-profit history only as a conditional reference; subtract the identified disposal gain and leave unallocated continuing and growth economics unquantified.

Treat operating cash, working capital, and debt as a conditional enterprise boundary. Do not call long-lived spending maintenance capital or infer a numeric owner-cash point estimate from the consolidated cash-flow history.

## 永久损失与最强反方

Focus on the risk that recurring operations remain cash-absorbing and that retained project capital lacks demonstrated returns; the group can continue refinancing while ordinary-share owner economics deteriorate.

No named rival is supplied; the relevant strongest rival is the unidentified television/display and supply-chain competitor set whose relative unit economics are not disclosed.

## 各项判断的投资含义

- The package identifies the operating businesses and reports consolidated adjusted attributable losses, but it does not allocate earnings, operating cash flow, working capital, capital spending, or debt across the continuing components. 因此，Continuing operations can support only a conditional route and cannot supply a base numerical normal-earnings range. 需要重估这一处理的条件是：Official responsibility-matched disclosures show sustained component earnings and cash conversion through a normal operating cycle. 该组件的权威经济去向为：正常盈利只进入条件范围；普通股现金只进入条件范围；对融资压力的影响取决于条件；永久损失进入基准永久损失路径；估值只作为条件性主要估值输入。晋级检验：Disclose and demonstrate responsibility-matched recurring earnings and cash conversion for the continuing operating perimeter. 失效检验：Continuing operations remain loss-making or consume cash after responsibility-matched working-capital movements over a normal cycle.
- Consolidated operating cash flow deteriorated to a FY2017 outflow that the report attributes to higher receivables and unexpired notes; inventory and short-term borrowing also rose across the reported periods. 因此，This component is a conditional owner-cash and financing-pressure input to the continuing-operations route, rather than proof of a normalized owner-cash amount. 需要重估这一处理的条件是：Official disclosures reconcile continuing-component receivables, inventory, debt, and operating cash through a sustained cash-conversion period. 该组件的权威经济去向为：正常盈利不适用；普通股现金只进入条件范围；增加融资压力；永久损失进入基准永久损失路径；估值只作为条件性主要估值输入。晋级检验：Reconcile receivables, inventory, financing, and operating cash to continuing components and demonstrate sustained conversion without debt dependence. 失效检验：Receivable or inventory absorption and refinancing needs continue to rise relative to continuing operating cash generation.
- FY2017 investment income of RMB 6.5878 billion from disposal of 70% of 康侨佳城 was identified in the annual report as non-sustainable, while disposal cash is a transaction-specific cash-flow fact. 因此，The disposal is removed from recurring earnings and owner-cash treatment; it may be monitored only as a nonrecurring liquidity event. 需要重估这一处理的条件是：A separate, disclosed recurring operating claim replaces the disposed-item gain without relying on sale proceeds. 该组件的权威经济去向为：正常盈利排除；普通股现金排除；对融资压力的影响取决于条件；永久损失进入条件性永久损失路径；估值排除。晋级检验：A separately evidenced recurring economic stream, rather than the sale proceeds, establishes a continuing owner-cash claim. 失效检验：The case uses the disposal gain or disposal cash as recurring earnings or recurring owner cash.
- The annual report names property and industrial-park projects and certain group investment amounts, but does not attribute project cash spending, financing, incremental earnings, or owner cash to the television/display or other operating components. 因此，Growth retention cannot enter normal earnings, owner cash, or a primary route until matched capital-and-return evidence exists. 需要重估这一处理的条件是：Official project reporting matches invested capital and financing to subsequent incremental earnings, cash conversion, and ordinary-share owner cash by responsibility boundary. 该组件的权威经济去向为：正常盈利尚未形成可承保范围；普通股现金尚未形成可承保范围；对融资压力的影响取决于条件；永久损失进入条件性永久损失路径；估值尚未形成可承保范围。晋级检验：Match project-by-project capital, financing, incremental earnings, and ordinary-share owner cash within a disclosed responsibility boundary. 失效检验：Material project capital remains unmatched to subsequent incremental earnings or cash while financing pressure persists.
