# 浙江爱仕达电器股份有限公司：企业承保读本

The company has a disclosed cookware-heavy branded consumer-products base and a rising FY2015-FY2017 consolidated recurring-earnings reference, but the route to ordinary-share owner cash is constrained by working-capital growth, all long-lived-asset spending and increased financing. New robotics, logistics and new-energy capital is an unresolved, separable risk rather than a base growth contribution.

## 行业未来与公司传导

观察时域：Medium term after the 2018-12-31 cutoff, assessed only from FY2015-FY2017 disclosed operating structure and financial observations.

最可能的行业路径：A branded, channel-dependent cookware and household-products business can preserve a meaningful operating base, but profit-pool durability is mixed because the source identifies a co-leading rival and does not disclose customer terms, component margins, or component cash conversion.

利润池传导：Domestic brand, supermarket, distributor and e-commerce access can transmit consumer demand into reported revenue, while receivables, inventory, long-lived-asset spending and financing can absorb the cash that would otherwise reach ordinary shareholders. New robotics and new-energy investments add a separate capital-allocation path whose return is not disclosed.

公司暴露：The product structure is disclosed as cookware-heavy, with low small-appliance revenue, and domestic cookware leadership is explicitly shared with 苏泊尔; the consolidated perimeter also contains robotics, logistics and new-energy entities without component cash or debt allocation.

适应能力：The company has disclosed domestic own-brand operations, OEM supply to international brands, overseas own-brand sales, an established domestic marketing network and broad e-commerce coverage. These are channels and position evidence, not evidence that expansion capital earns an adequate return.

正常经济与普通股现金：FY2015-FY2017 consolidated revenue and net profit excluding non-recurring gains and losses increased, supporting a current recurring-earnings reference. The absence of component profitability and unit economics prevents attributing that reference to a durable consumer-products margin or to the newer components.

永久损失路径：The material risk is slow value destruction rather than a stated immediate failure: rising working-capital balances, long-lived-asset spending and debt can consume cash, while unproven robotics/new-energy investment can retain capital without demonstrated owner return.

价值处理：Use only a price-free recurring-earnings route conditioned on owner-cash conversion and financing pressure; do not give a separate growth route to robotics or new energy until component-level capital and return evidence is available.

最强竞争解释：苏泊尔：FY2017 source identifies it, alongside the company, as a leading domestic cookware brand, particularly in the supermarket channel.

## 行业处境、公司位置与适应

The reported company combines a cookware-heavy consumer-products base, domestic brand and retail-channel coverage, OEM and overseas sales, with new robotics, logistics and new-energy entities. Revenue and deducted net profit rose through FY2017, but the source also shows rising receivables, inventory, long-lived-asset cash spending and borrowings, so reported profit growth does not establish distributable owner cash or the economics of the new components.

The observable base is a cookware-heavy consumer-products business with domestic own-brand, OEM, overseas distribution, supermarket and e-commerce channels. The position has disclosed brand and channel reach, but it shares domestic cookware leadership with 苏泊尔 and the source does not supply customer concentration, pricing, margin or segment cash evidence.

Brand, marketing-network, distributor, retailer-quality-management and e-commerce disclosures support an adaptation capability for the consumer-products base. The creation and acquisition of robotics, logistics and new-energy entities shows expansion activity, but the supplied record does not demonstrate their component return on retained capital or their cash needs.

## 生存、正常盈利与普通股现金

FY2017 short-term borrowings of RMB 694.0 million and long-term borrowings of RMB 278.56 million, together with pledged fixed and intangible assets, show increased financing dependence relative to the located FY2016 short-term-borrowing figure. The source does not establish an immediate solvency break, but it also does not allocate debt, collateral or cash generation by component; survival is therefore monitored through financing pressure rather than assumed from consolidated profit.

RMB 142,556,215.23 of FY2017 attributable net profit excluding non-recurring gains and losses is a disclosed consolidated after-tax recurring-earnings reference. It is not re-labelled as owner cash: FY2017 operating cash flow was RMB 107,125,052.73 while purchases of fixed, intangible and other long-lived assets were RMB 187,331,471.04, and the source does not distinguish maintenance spending from growth spending or allocate either item by component.

Use only the disclosed FY2017 consolidated profit excluding non-recurring gains and losses as a reference range. Do not infer consumer-products segment margins or add a return from new components without responsibility-matched evidence.

Treat owner cash as conditional: operating cash less all long-lived-asset spending shows current capital-allocation and financing pressure, but it is not economic maintenance capital and cannot establish a negative owner-cash point estimate.

## 永久损失与最强反方

Include slow erosion through cash absorption, debt and unproven retained expansion capital, as well as competitive pressure from the named cookware rival; do not restrict permanent loss to a near-term solvency event.

苏泊尔：FY2017 source identifies it, alongside the company, as a leading domestic cookware brand, particularly in the supermarket channel.

## 价值路线与当前处理

The active route is a price-free treatment of the disclosed consolidated recurring-earnings reference, conditional on whether operating cash can cover the economic capital required by the business and its financing burden. A separate growth route for robotics and new energy is unresolved because the supplied source has no component cash, capital, debt or return evidence.

Keep the recurring-earnings route price-free and conditional on owner-cash conversion and financing; exclude a separate robotics/new-energy growth route until component-level return and cash evidence exists.

- FY2017 attributable net profit excluding non-recurring gains and losses is a disclosed after-tax consolidated reference, and the disclosed product mix is cookware-heavy.
