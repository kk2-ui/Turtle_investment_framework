# 浙江爱仕达电器股份有限公司：企业承保读本

The cookware-weighted incumbent has a usable three-year adjusted consolidated earnings record and channel reach, but the cash route is weaker and conditional because working capital, long-lived-asset spending and debt rose or deteriorated in aggregate. New robot, new-energy and logistics capital receives no incremental return credit until its economics are evidenced.

## 行业未来与公司传导

观察时域：Post-FY2017 operating normalization

最可能的行业路径：A mature, channel-diverse consumer-durables core in which reported sales growth is useful only when it converts through margins and working capital; separately funded expansion must earn its way into the enterprise case.

利润池传导：The disclosed revenue and adjusted-profit increase can support incumbent earning power, but receivables, inventory, long-lived-asset spending and borrowing determine whether the same growth reaches ordinary-share cash rather than absorbing financing capacity.

公司暴露：The company is cookware-weighted, uses domestic branded sales, international OEM and overseas branded sales, and reaches both traditional retailers and named online platforms; its consolidated data do not allocate cash, working capital or financing to those channels or products.

适应能力：The disclosed marketing network, online-platform coverage and international-retailer quality relationships show channel access, while the record does not establish product- or channel-level price, margin, collection or return economics.

正常经济与普通股现金：The FY2015-FY2017 adjusted-profit record is usable as a bounded incumbent consolidated reference, not as proof that every product line or new project has the same economics.

永久损失路径：The principal slow-loss path is retaining or borrowing capital for working-capital absorption and new projects whose matched cash return is undisclosed, while a rival can contest the cookware profit pool through brand and channel execution.

价值处理：Keep incumbent earning power distinct from the conditional owner-cash route and exclude unmeasured robot, new-energy and logistics incremental returns from any resolved route.

最强竞争解释：苏泊尔；FY2017 披露将其与爱仕达同列为炊具内销领导品牌，并称爱仕达在商超渠道有品牌优势。

## 行业处境、公司位置与适应

The reported business is cookware-weighted, sold through domestic branding, overseas distributors and expanding online channels. Consolidated revenue and adjusted profit rose through FY2017, but cash conversion and financing pressure weakened while new robot, new-energy and logistics investments lack responsibility-matched operating evidence.

Cookware is the stated product emphasis; the company reports domestic-brand leadership alongside 苏泊尔 in domestic cookware, domestic marketing coverage, overseas distributors, OEM relationships and online-platform coverage. Those facts establish position and channel exposure, not a product-level margin or collection advantage.

Channel breadth and the declared move into robot, new-energy and logistics entities are adaptation attempts. Their operating success is not inferred from implementation or funding because the package provides no component-level customer acceptance, earnings, cash, working-capital or return bridge.

## 生存、正常盈利与普通股现金

FY2017 ended with short-term borrowings of RMB 694.0 million, long-term borrowings of RMB 278.56 million and pledged assets, after reported operating cash flow fell from FY2016 while receivables and inventory increased. This is a financing-pressure case rather than evidence that the mature core has ceased operating; allocation by component and maintenance-versus-growth spending remain unobserved.

Reference normal earnings to the FY2015-FY2017 range of reported adjusted profit attributable to shareholders. Government subsidies, investment/foreign-exchange gains and relocation expense show why unadjusted FY2017 net profit is not the reference; no segment disclosure permits assigning the reference range to individual products or projects.

Use only the bounded FY2015-FY2017 adjusted-profit reference for incumbent consolidated earning power; do not convert revenue growth, unadjusted net profit or disclosed new-project funding into an additional normal-earnings claim.

Operating cash less economic maintenance capital is unknown. Operating cash less all disclosed long-lived-asset spending is retained solely as current capital-allocation and financing-pressure evidence, while the subsequent return on growth capital is unresolved.

## 永久损失与最强反方

Treat low-return retention, working-capital absorption, debt dependence and an unproven expansion return as conditional slow-erosion paths; do not confuse continued operations or consolidated cash flow with established ordinary-share owner cash.

## 价值路线与当前处理

The incumbent route is limited to the disclosed adjusted consolidated earnings history. The owner-cash route is conditional because operating cash, all long-lived-asset spending, working capital and borrowing are disclosed only in aggregate and do not distinguish economic maintenance from growth capital. The financing-pressure route is stress-only, and the new-project route remains unresolved rather than being blended into incumbent economics.

Retain the incumbent earning-power route and a separate conditional owner-cash route; the financing route is stress-only and the robot, new-energy and logistics route is unresolved rather than blended into incumbent economics.
