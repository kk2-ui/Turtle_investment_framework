# 广东新宝电器股份有限公司：企业承保读本

The best current judgment is a viable mature small-appliance core with a disclosed, bounded three-year adjusted-profit history, tempered by raw-material, currency, and customer-bargaining exposure. Owner cash and retained-growth value cannot be collapsed into that core because maintenance capital, cash accessibility, and incremental return are not established.

## 行业未来与公司传导

观察时域：Medium-term continuation from the 2018-12-31 cutoff

最可能的行业路径：A competitive export small-appliance manufacturing regime in which product breadth, design-to-production execution, input costs, currency, and customer bargaining jointly determine retained profit rather than volume growth alone.

利润池传导：Higher sales and broader product lines can raise factory utilization and profit, but raw-material inflation and exchange-rate-related finance costs can absorb the gain; concentration in the FY2015 top five customers makes customer terms a material transmission channel, while later concentration is not disclosed in the supplied package.

公司暴露：Small appliances represented 98.15% of FY2015 revenue and overseas sales 88.06%, making consolidated earnings exposed to export demand, customer purchasing behavior, product mix, input costs, and currency effects. The supplied package does not split later profit or cash by product, customer, ODM, or domestic-brand channel.

适应能力：The company disclosed a one-stop offering from market strategy and design through molds, certification, testing, and mass-production technology, and attributed FY2016 profit growth partly to wider product lines, innovation products, and automation. These are credible adaptation actions, but their durable unit-economic advantage is not separately measured.

正常经济与普通股现金：MIXED: reported revenue expanded from CNY 6.31bn to CNY 8.22bn and adjusted attributable profit remained positive across all three years, but FY2017 adjusted profit fell from FY2016 and disclosed input-cost and currency pressures prevent extrapolating the FY2016 peak.

永久损失路径：The principal permanent-loss path is slow erosion: customers or competing suppliers pressure economics while retained capital, working capital, and long-lived-asset spending fail to earn disclosed incremental returns. A second path is that consolidated cash is less available to ordinary shareholders than its headline amount suggests because restricted balances, allocation, and distribution evidence are incomplete.

价值处理：Keep a mature-core earning-power route separate from a conditional owner-cash route. Do not recognize a standalone growth-capital-return route and do not treat all consolidated cash as an ordinary-shareholder asset without an accessibility and realization boundary.

最强竞争解释：Global branded customers and competing small-appliance OEM suppliers can shift volume or price; client-specific concentration after FY2015 is unknown.

## 行业处境、公司位置与适应

The disclosed company is an export-oriented small-appliance designer, manufacturer, and seller with a broad product set and an integrated customer solution offer. Revenue grew across FY2015–FY2017, but FY2017 cost growth exceeded revenue growth amid raw-material inflation and financial expense rose with RMB/USD movements; the pre-cutoff record therefore supports a viable core but not a claim of steadily improving economics.

The company described itself as a Chinese export leader in small appliances and offered a broad product range plus an integrated design-to-mass-production solution. That position supports a meaningful operating perimeter, but disclosed FY2015 top-five-customer sales of 38.21% and absent later customer data leave bargaining-power durability unbounded.

Product-line expansion, innovation products, automation, and the end-to-end customer solution are observable company actions. They offset neither the disclosed FY2017 raw-material-cost and currency headwinds nor the absent evidence on channel-specific cash conversion and incremental return.

## 生存、正常盈利与普通股现金

The FY2017 balance shows CNY 1.917bn of cash and cash equivalents against disclosed restricted monetary funds of CNY 199m; working-capital balances and long-lived-asset spending are material, and FY2017 also received CNY 891m of investment cash. The supplied reports do not provide a complete later debt or ordinary-shareholder cash-accessibility analysis, so liquidity appears supportive but not a full distributability claim.

Use the FY2015–FY2017 adjusted attributable-profit range of CNY 272.4m–412.4m as a disclosed historical reference for the mature consolidated core. Do not promote the FY2016 high to a trend, and do not add unmeasured project or acquisition returns.

Use only the disclosed FY2015–FY2017 adjusted attributable-profit range as the core reference; keep unproved growth return unresolved and do not infer a trend from the FY2016 peak.

Treat owner cash as conditional: operating cash, all long-lived-asset spending, working-capital commitments, cash restrictions, and ordinary-shareholder realization answer distinct questions, and the source does not identify economic maintenance capital.

## 永久损失与最强反方

Include both survival-independent slow erosion from competitive/cost pressure and low-return retention, plus a conditional loss path from inaccessible or absorbed cash; liquidity does not eliminate these paths.

Global branded customers and competing small-appliance OEM suppliers can shift volume or price; client-specific concentration after FY2015 is unknown.

## 价值路线与当前处理

The core route relies only on the disclosed consolidated adjusted-profit history. The owner-cash route is conditional on separating maintenance from growth capital and establishing cash accessibility. The full-consolidated-cash route is excluded because restriction, allocation, and ordinary-shareholder realization are not fully evidenced. The unproved growth-capital route remains unresolved rather than being added to core earnings.

Maintain separate mature-core and conditional-owner-cash routes, exclude full consolidated cash as an ordinary-owner route, and leave growth-capital return unresolved rather than embedding it in the base.

## 各项判断的投资含义

- The company’s small-appliance operating perimeter, product/service offer, and three-year consolidated revenue and adjusted-profit observations are directly disclosed; FY2017 cost and currency pressure is also disclosed and belongs inside the bounded historical core rather than being ignored. 因此，This component supplies the mature-core normal-earnings reference and the core earning-power route, but it does not establish a forward margin trend or channel-level cash conversion. 需要重估这一处理的条件是：Promote confidence in durable economics only if later official disclosures show sustained adjusted-profit conversion under input-cost and currency pressure on a comparable consolidated perimeter. 该组件的权威经济去向为：正常盈利进入基准范围；普通股现金只进入条件范围；不改变融资压力；永久损失进入基准永久损失路径；估值作为主要估值输入。晋级检验：Later official reports sustain comparable adjusted-profit conversion through raw-material and currency pressure. 失效检验：Comparable disclosures show that customer, input-cost, or currency pressure causes persistent earnings erosion outside the disclosed historical range.
- The reports disclose material long-lived-asset cash payments and receivable, inventory, and payable balances, but do not separate economic maintenance capital from growth capital or attribute cash flow by channel or project. 因此，All long-lived-asset spending and working-capital commitment inform current financing pressure; ordinary-owner cash is conditional and no negative owner-cash point estimate is inferred from total spending. 需要重估这一处理的条件是：Resolve with maintenance-versus-growth capital evidence, project-level or channel-level cash conversion, and matched incremental earnings after working-capital absorption. 该组件的权威经济去向为：正常盈利不适用；普通股现金只进入条件范围；对融资压力的影响取决于条件；永久损失进入条件性永久损失路径；估值只作为条件性主要估值输入。晋级检验：Disclosures separate maintenance from growth capital and show working-capital-normalized owner cash. 失效检验：Matched disclosures show that recurring capital and working-capital absorption persistently consume operating cash without adequate incremental return.
- Cash and restricted monetary funds are disclosed, as are FY2017 investment cash receipts and project funding balances, but the source package does not establish the allocation, upstreamability, or ordinary-shareholder realization of all consolidated cash. 因此，Liquidity mitigates survival pressure, but the owner-cash route remains conditional; treating every consolidated cash unit as an ordinary-shareholder asset is expressly excluded. 需要重估这一处理的条件是：Resolve through legal-entity cash ownership, restriction release, allocation, distribution history, and post-distribution balance evidence. 该组件的权威经济去向为：正常盈利不适用；普通股现金只进入条件范围；降低融资压力；永久损失进入条件性永久损失路径；估值只作为条件性主要估值输入。晋级检验：Entity-level ownership, restrictions, allocation, and recurring ordinary-shareholder realization establish an accessible cash boundary. 失效检验：Disclosures show cash restrictions, financing dependence, or allocation needs materially greater than the current conditional treatment permits.
- Technology projects, automation, a non-public offering, and an acquisition are disclosed, but the supplied evidence does not match their capital to subsequent incremental earnings, cash conversion, or returns. 因此，No growth return is added to normal earnings, owner cash, or a primary route; the effect remains an unresolved permanent-loss and financing consideration. 需要重估这一处理的条件是：Resolve only with responsibility-matched disclosures of capital deployed, incremental earnings, maintenance needs, working-capital absorption, and ordinary-owner cash realization. 该组件的权威经济去向为：正常盈利尚未形成可承保范围；普通股现金尚未形成可承保范围；对融资压力的影响取决于条件；永久损失尚未形成可承保范围；估值尚未形成可承保范围。晋级检验：Responsibility-matched evidence establishes incremental return and owner-cash conversion after all capital absorption. 失效检验：Matched capital and later results demonstrate structurally inadequate incremental return or irreversible cash absorption.
