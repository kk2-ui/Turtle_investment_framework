# 华意压缩机股份有限公司：企业承保读本

The best-current price-free judgment is a mature compressor engine whose disclosed revenue growth did not prevent a FY2017 collapse in recurring attributable profit under raw-material pressure. Group cash conversion and financing pressure make owner cash conditional, while unsegmented acquisitions and new businesses cannot be granted a growth contribution.

## 行业未来与公司传导

观察时域：The next reporting cycles after the 2018-12-31 cutoff.

最可能的行业路径：A mature compressor supply regime in which volume and geographic expansion can coexist with raw-material pressure, customer bargaining, and working-capital absorption.

利润池传导：For a design-and-production compressor supplier, raw-material cost pressure and the ability to recover it through customers transmit through recurring compressor profit; inventory funding and supplier-payment timing then transmit into aggregate operating cash and short-term financing need.

公司暴露：The company supplies globally known refrigerator enterprises, earns its disclosed main business from refrigerator and commercial compressors, and reports that Jiaxipera is its main profit source; the supplied reports do not disclose customer contracts, concentration, or component-level cash conversion.

适应能力：The company has three compressor brands and reported efforts to stabilize Europe and develop the Americas, Southeast Asia, and domestic markets, plus new non-compressor activities. Those actions show breadth of response but do not establish separately observable unit returns or cash conversion for the new activities.

正常经济与普通股现金：The factual three-year recurring-profit record is 184.97 million CNY in FY2015, 203.18 million CNY in FY2016, and 60.29 million CNY in FY2017. It supports a broad historical core-earnings reference rather than a claim that the FY2017 decline is fully temporary.

永久损失路径：The relevant loss path is not only solvency: continuing input-cost pressure, inventory-funded cash absorption, and capital retained in unsegmented acquisitions or projects could erode per-share owner economics even if the group continues to finance itself.

价值处理：Use only a route anchored in the mature compressor earnings record with conditional treatment for aggregate owner-cash conversion; exclude unsegmented acquisition and new-business growth until responsibility-matched earnings, cash, and capital data are disclosed.

最强竞争解释：Unresolved named rival: the supplied reports name no competitor; the strongest evidenced competitive challenge is alternative compressor supply to global refrigerator OEMs combined with customer bargaining under raw-material pressure.

## 行业处境、公司位置与适应

The disclosed enterprise is a production-led refrigerator and commercial-compressor supplier with global refrigerator-OEM exposure. Revenue grew through FY2017, but recurring attributable profit fell sharply as major raw-material costs rose; aggregate operating cash turned negative while inventory and short-term borrowings increased.

The disclosed core position is a multi-brand compressor manufacturer and important supplier to globally known refrigerator enterprises. Jiaxipera is identified as the main profit source, but the reports do not disclose component-level customer economics or cash conversion.

Geographic market development, three compressor brands, commercial-compressor activity, and newly acquired or added businesses are documented adaptations. Only the mature compressor business has a disclosed central operating role; implementation of new businesses is not evidence of their mature return on capital.

## 生存、正常盈利与普通股现金

FY2017 group cash generation and financing data indicate pressure rather than an established failure: operating cash flow was negative, inventory rose, and short-term borrowings rose to 2.021 billion CNY; group borrowing inflows and repayments show financing activity but cannot be allocated among components.

Use the FY2015-FY2017 attributable profit after non-recurring items as a 60.29-203.18 million CNY historical reference for the mature compressor engine. Treat the FY2017 raw-material explanation as a pressure observation, not proof of a fully reversible adjustment, and exclude separately unobservable non-operating and new-business contributions from base normalization.

Anchor normal earnings only to the 60.29-203.18 million CNY FY2015-FY2017 recurring attributable-profit reference, with no assertion that FY2017 is automatically temporary and no separate new-business contribution.

Treat owner cash as conditional and consolidated: FY2017 negative operating cash, inventory growth, short-term borrowing growth, and restricted assets establish pressure, but the evidence cannot assign operating cash, maintenance capital, growth capital, or financing to the compressor engine.

## 永久损失与最强反方

Permanent-loss analysis includes slow erosion from unrecovered cost pressure, working-capital and financing absorption, and unsupported returns on acquisition or project capital; it is not limited to immediate insolvency.

Unresolved named rival: the supplied reports name no competitor; the strongest evidenced competitive challenge is alternative compressor supply to global refrigerator OEMs combined with customer bargaining under raw-material pressure.

## 价值路线与当前处理

The sole primary model role is a mature-compressor earnings route. It carries a conditional owner-cash boundary because cash flow, capex, working capital, and borrowings are disclosed only for the group. The excluded routes preserve the acquisition/new-business and non-operating residuals as explicit non-base questions rather than imputing them into the compressor route.

The primary route is mature compressor normal earnings with a conditional consolidated owner-cash boundary. Non-compressor acquisitions/new businesses and non-operating financial items stay in explicitly excluded or unresolved routes until their economics and shareholder realization are evidenced.

## 各项判断的投资含义

- Refrigerator and commercial compressors are the disclosed main business; FY2017 identifies Jiaxipera as the main profit source and supplies a three-year recurring attributable-profit record. The reports support a mature-engine earnings reference but not a disaggregated cash-flow calculation. 因此，The mature compressor engine can anchor the price-free normal-earnings route, while owner-cash recognition remains conditional on group-level cash and financing evidence. 需要重估这一处理的条件是：Promote owner-cash treatment from conditional to base only when official disclosures reconcile compressor cash conversion, working capital, maintenance versus growth spending, and financing to the same component boundary. 该组件的权威经济去向为：正常盈利进入基准范围；普通股现金只进入条件范围；对融资压力的影响取决于条件；永久损失进入基准永久损失路径；估值作为主要估值输入。晋级检验：Official disclosures reconcile compressor-level operating cash, working capital, maintenance spending, growth spending, and financing over a comparable period. 失效检验：Official post-cutoff reports show that the recurring compressor earnings reference was materially supported by non-core income or that compressor economics deteriorate despite easing input pressure.
- FY2017 reports negative operating cash flow, a large inventory increase, higher short-term borrowings, restricted assets, and group-level borrowing flows. The same reports explicitly do not allocate these items among compressor and non-compressor components. 因此，This component constrains owner-cash and financing-pressure conclusions on a conditional consolidated basis; it does not authorize a component-specific maintenance-capital estimate. 需要重估这一处理的条件是：Resolve to a base owner-cash range only when the issuer supplies responsibility-matched cash flow, working capital, long-lived spending, and borrowings for the mature compressor engine. 该组件的权威经济去向为：正常盈利不适用；普通股现金只进入条件范围；增加融资压力；永久损失进入基准永久损失路径；估值只作为条件性主要估值输入。晋级检验：A component-level disclosure demonstrates that the mature compressor engine converts earnings to operating cash after its own working-capital and long-lived-asset needs. 失效检验：Official reports show that inventory, cash-flow, and borrowing pressure is persistently worse at the mature compressor responsibility boundary than the consolidated figures suggest.
- Trade, technical services, automotive-air-conditioning compressors, and cleaning robots are disclosed as new business; Shanghai Weile and Greebo acquisitions have disclosed investment amounts, but the supplied reports provide no component-level earnings, cash, working-capital, capex, or borrowing allocation. 因此，No incremental earnings, owner-cash, return-on-growth-capital, or primary-route contribution is inferred for these businesses. 需要重估这一处理的条件是：Resolve only after official disclosures link each material business or acquisition to revenue, profit, working capital, cumulative capital absorbed, and cash generation on the same responsibility boundary. 该组件的权威经济去向为：正常盈利尚未形成可承保范围；普通股现金尚未形成可承保范围；尚未形成可承保范围；永久损失尚未形成可承保范围；估值尚未形成可承保范围。晋级检验：Official component data link capital invested in each acquired or new business to sustained earnings, cash generation, working capital, and incremental return. 失效检验：Official disclosures show further material capital absorption or financing dependence without responsibility-matched earnings and cash evidence.
- FY2017 reports investment income from bank wealth-management products, investee returns, and forward-exchange business, alongside a negative fair-value change on forward contracts. The supplied package does not establish these as repeatable operating earnings or ordinary-share owner cash. 因此，These disclosed items remain outside the base earnings, owner-cash, and primary-route treatment. 需要重估这一处理的条件是：Reconsider only if official disclosures establish recurring, attributable, cash-realized economics and a shareholder realization path distinct from the compressor operating engine. 该组件的权威经济去向为：正常盈利排除；普通股现金排除；不适用；永久损失排除；估值排除。晋级检验：Official disclosures demonstrate recurring cash realization and ordinary-shareholder accessibility independent of one-off financial-market movements. 失效检验：Official disclosures show that financial-product or foreign-exchange results are being relied upon to support recurring operating earnings.
