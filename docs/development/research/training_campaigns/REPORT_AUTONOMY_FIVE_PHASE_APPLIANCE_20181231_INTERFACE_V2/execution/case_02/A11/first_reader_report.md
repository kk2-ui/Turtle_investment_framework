# 华意压缩机股份有限公司：企业承保读本

The best-supported current judgment is a compressor-centered mature operating engine with an established customer and brand position, but a FY2017 deterioration in adjusted earnings and cash conversion makes working-capital funding conditional and prevents newer businesses from being treated as core value creation.

## 行业未来与公司传导

观察时域：Medium-term normalization after FY2017

最可能的行业路径：A mature, customer-qualified refrigeration-compressor market in which input-cost pressure and customer supply requirements can transmit quickly into producer margins and working-capital needs.

利润池传导：For a production supplier, raw-material inflation can compress earnings unless recovered through customers; higher sales can also require inventory and supplier cash before receipts convert to operating cash.

公司暴露：The company is a design, production, and sales supplier of refrigerator and commercial compressors to well-known global refrigerator customers; FY2017 states that major raw-material price increases reduced group and subsidiary profits.

适应能力：The disclosed adaptation is geographic customer management and a portfolio of HUAYI, JIAXIPERA, and CUBIGEL compressor brands, with Europe stabilized and the Americas, Southeast Asia, and domestic markets developed; the record does not establish that this offsets input-cost or cash-conversion pressure.

正常经济与普通股现金：The established compressor business is the supported earning engine because Jiaxipera is identified as the main profit source, but the FY2015--FY2017 consolidated adjusted-profit range is volatile and cannot be assigned precisely between compressor subsegments and newer businesses.

永久损失路径：The principal non-bankruptcy path is persistent margin pressure and cash absorption through inventory and short-term borrowing, compounded if acquisition and new-business spending earns inadequate cash returns; the disclosed core customer and brand position provides a counterweight but not a proof of recovery.

价值处理：Treat only the established compressor-centered operating earning-power route as primary; retain working-capital and financing effects as conditional and exclude non-operating gains while leaving new acquisitions unresolved until component cash and return evidence exists.

最强竞争解释：The strongest rival is a competing refrigerator-compressor supplier able to match global customer qualification and price while not carrying the same raw-material, working-capital, and non-core capital burden.

## 行业处境、公司位置与适应

Revenue rose through FY2017, but adjusted profit and operating cash conversion weakened as disclosed raw-material costs, inventory funding, and short-term borrowing increased; the available record supports a core compressor franchise but not a component-level cash or return allocation for newer activities.

The enterprise has a disclosed global refrigerator-customer supplier role and three compressor brands, with Jiaxipera identified as the main profit source; this is a meaningful operating position, though the source does not provide customer concentration, contract terms, or component-level economics.

Customer-market expansion and the compressor-brand portfolio are the disclosed adaptation. Acquisitions of automotive-air-conditioning and cleaning-robot businesses are separate activities whose cash requirements and returns are not allocated in the allowed record.

## 生存、正常盈利与普通股现金

FY2017 had no reported long-term borrowings, but short-term borrowings rose to CNY 2.021 billion and operating cash flow was negative while inventory increased. Survival is therefore not disproved, but current funding pressure must remain explicit and cannot be inferred away from the absence of long-term debt.

Use the FY2016 and FY2017 adjusted profits as a bounded, after-tax consolidated reference for the compressor-centered enterprise, then exclude non-operating gains and preserve the lack of component-level allocation instead of treating FY2017's low result or new-business spending as a precise steady state.

Use the FY2016-FY2017 after-tax adjusted-profit interval as a broad reference for the mature compressor-centered enterprise. Exclude non-operating gains and do not allocate unreported acquisition or new-business results into the base.

Separate operating cash less economic maintenance capital from operating cash less all long-lived-asset spending. The allowed record supplies total cash-flow and spending data but not maintenance-versus-growth or component allocation, so current cash pressure is conditional rather than a fabricated owner-cash point estimate.

## 永久损失与最强反方

Treat sustained cost pressure, inventory-funded cash absorption, short-term borrowing dependence, and low-return non-core retention as material slow-erosion paths; do not equate the absence of long-term borrowing with a low permanent-loss path.

The strongest rival is a competing refrigerator-compressor supplier able to match global customer qualification and price while not carrying the same raw-material, working-capital, and non-core capital burden.

## 价值路线与当前处理

The primary route uses only the disclosed mature compressor-centered earning engine. It does not convert unallocated cash spending, acquisition outlays, or non-operating gains into core owner economics. The working-capital component is optional and conditional because its group cash effect is material but not component allocated.

The primary route is established compressor operating earning power. Working-capital and financing demands are conditional; acquisitions and new businesses remain unresolved, and non-operating investment and foreign-exchange effects are excluded.
