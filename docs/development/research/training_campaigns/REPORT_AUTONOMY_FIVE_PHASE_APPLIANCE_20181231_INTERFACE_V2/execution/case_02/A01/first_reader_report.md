## 行业未来与公司传导

观察时域：Near-to-medium term following the 2018-12-31 cutoff.

最可能的行业路径：The supplied company disclosures support a mixed demand-and-cost regime: established compressor markets and global customers coexist with raw-material pressure, inventory build, and unproven new activities; they do not establish a company-specific volume or pricing outlook.

利润池传导：For the disclosed compressor operations, customer absorption and product mix must pass through raw-material costs, inventory funding, receivables collection, and production investment before they can become consolidated ordinary earnings or cash generation.

公司暴露：The company produces refrigerator/freezer and commercial compressors and serves global refrigerator enterprises through HUAYI, JIAXIPERA, and CUBIGEL brands, while newly consolidated automotive-parts and cleaning-robot activities sit outside the demonstrated compressor perimeter.

适应能力：The disclosed adaptation is geographic customer development and three compressor brands; no supplied component-level evidence measures price realization, unit cost, customer retention, or cash conversion from that adaptation.

正常经济与普通股现金：Reported adjusted attributable profit fell from CNY 203.18 million in FY2016 to CNY 60.29 million in FY2017 as the report cited higher major raw-material prices; the reports do not allocate that deterioration among refrigerator, commercial, and new-business components.

永久损失路径：The adverse path is that inventory funding, negative FY2017 operating cash flow, higher short-term borrowing, restricted assets, and unallocated acquisition/project commitments persist while core component cash generation is not demonstrated.

价值处理：Keep the consolidated reported earnings reference conditional, use financing pressure only as a stress route, and exclude noncore/acquisition value until component-level profitability, cash, and capital-return evidence is available.

最强竞争解释：No named rival is disclosed; the relevant rival is an unquantified global refrigerator-compressor competitor set.

- Refrigerator/freezer compressors are an explicitly disclosed core product with global refrigerator-company customers, but the package gives no component income, cash, working-capital, capex, or borrowing allocation. 因此，The core can inform the conditional consolidated route but cannot independently create a normal-earnings or owner-cash range. 需要重估这一处理的条件是：Obtain refrigerator-compressor component revenue, volume/price, gross-margin, operating-cash, working-capital, and capital-spending disclosures on the same perimeter. 该组件的权威经济去向为：正常盈利只进入条件范围；普通股现金尚未形成可承保范围；对融资压力的影响取决于条件；永久损失进入条件性永久损失路径；估值只作为条件性主要估值输入。晋级检验：Same-scope refrigerator-compressor earnings and cash evidence confirms the core is represented by the consolidated reference. 失效检验：Core product price, volume, unit cost, or cash conversion differs materially from the consolidated series.
