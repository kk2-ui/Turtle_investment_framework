康佳 remains a diversified, financing-dependent appliance and trading enterprise whose 2017 accounting profit is not a normal baseline; a conditional core route survives only if operating cash conversion and component economics improve and are independently disclosed.

## 行业处境、公司位置与适应

康佳 is a diversified appliance and trading group. Revenue expanded sharply by 2017, but reported attributable profit was dominated by a nonrecurring property disposal and operating cash flow was deeply negative, so enterprise claims must separate core operations from disposal proceeds and working-capital funding.

康佳 has broad domestic B2B/B2C and overseas B2B television channels, supply-chain trading in components and materials, and additional white-goods and mobile businesses. FY2017 multimedia was reorganized, but the source package does not provide responsibility-unit profitability, customer concentration, receivable ageing or component financing balances; competitive position is therefore conditional rather than established.

Multimedia integration and company-level sales organization are credible organizational adaptations. Supply-chain scale and industrial-park preparation may create options, but without unit economics, customer terms, or project-level cash attribution they remain conditional and cannot be promoted to base earning power.
