## Purpose

Round 7 tests whether Turtle gains investment utility by evaluating a company
as a connected operating system instead of one action, one narrative or one
financial trend. It compares a trend-based Baseline with an eight-dimension
Enhanced chain under the same company, cutoff, official source packet and
outcome measurement contract.

## Implementation

- `scripts/enterprise_judgment_round7_multidimensional.py` mechanically selects
  the next immutable roster transition, freezes both views and validates the
  full judgment chain.
- Existing Enterprise V3 acquisition and settlement adapters own outcome
  processing. Round 7 adds no second observation or settlement system.
- Each judgment has an explicit claim scope and dependent outcome cells, so
  issuer sales cannot support customer response and operating cash cannot
  support owner-cash claims.
- The external custodian field-record file is exact-cover validated before
  settlement. `UNKNOWN` and `MEASUREMENT_MISMATCH` propagate locally.
- The independent review must cover all eight dimensions in order and may use
  only canonical settled cells. The completion receipt grants no downstream
  research or investment rights.

## Real Result

The frozen transition is CN:600585 `2014-04-16 -> 2015-04-15`. The FY2014
official annual report produces 17 settled cells: nine observed, seven unknown
and one local measurement mismatch. The independent reviewer concludes that
Enhanced materially improves enterprise judgment in four dimensions, has no
material difference in three, and is not diagnostic in one.

The result supports a narrow investor statement: relative volume and funding
capacity improved, while organic execution, direct customer advantage,
durable unit economics, owner cash, adaptation and permanent-loss resilience
remain unproved. The method's observed value is reduced false attribution, not
an enterprise-quality score.

## Boundary

Repository history contains later company summaries, so this development turn
has no holdout score authority. It does not authorize general method transfer,
Comparative, CJO, valuation, report generation or investment action. Those
rights require a different company-period replication without later-summary
contamination and evidence that the changed method alters a material research
or investment conclusion.
