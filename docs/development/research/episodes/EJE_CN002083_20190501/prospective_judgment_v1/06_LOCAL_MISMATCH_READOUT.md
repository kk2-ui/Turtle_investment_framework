# CN:002083 FY2019 Source-clock Readout

The first prospective development episode remains economically unresolved.
The official CNINFO category query identified no eligible original full FY2019
annual report available by the contract's `2020-05-01` due date. The only
FY2019 full-report title returned was marked cancelled and dated 2020-06-30.
Its body was not opened and no outcome field was read.

This result says nothing about customers, margins, project execution, cash,
debt, management quality or permanent loss. It identifies a source-clock
mismatch created by the frozen deadline. The three pre-outcome judgments
remain frozen and `UNRESOLVED`; they are not weakened merely because custody
could not observe the contracted window.

The useful process lesson is narrow: a calendar-year annual-report outcome
contract with a fixed next-May due date can be censored by delayed publication.
That lesson does not justify a new global gate or a retrospective deadline
extension. The next development episode may use a different issuer selected
from the already registered H1 order, but it cannot count this mismatch as
enterprise feedback or prompt utility.
