# Paired Prompt V1 Diagnostic — Invalid for Learning Attribution

Two fresh `gpt-5.6-sol / high` contexts were run once with no outcome access.
Baseline received only `01_COMMON_PREOUTCOME_EVIDENCE_PACKET.md`; Enhanced
received that packet plus `02_SOURCE_LEARNING_NOTE_ENHANCED_ONLY.md`.

## Result

Both arms independently made economic absorption, rather than another build
milestone, the central question. Both required product volume, margin,
inventory, cash capex and borrowing to settle capital-allocation credit. Both
kept construction credit separate from economic-effect credit and proposed
FY2018 comparators for FY2019 trend claims.

The Enhanced arm was more explicit about project-specific production, sales
and margin fields and about the named learning lineage. That is useful but not
a material pre-outcome judgment difference by itself.

## Why this run cannot decide transfer

The common packet itself ended by stating that the decisive question was
whether new overseas capacity was economically absorbed. That sentence is a
task-level version of `LR:CN002394:ECONOMIC_ABSORPTION_AFTER_BUILD`; the
Baseline therefore was not a clean representation of the pre-learning method.
The run is retained as a design diagnosis and is not repaired through repeated
prompting.

The valid Baseline for transfer attribution is instead the actual pre-feedback
measurement policy committed for CN:002394: current-period levels plus a build
milestone, without systematically registered same-definition comparators or
post-build project economics. The next artifacts mechanically project that
policy to CN:000726 and compare it with the learned policy. This uses actual
serial method state rather than inventing a deliberately weak writer.

## Defensive-writing audit

`PASS` for both arm outputs: neither used causal limits or `UNKNOWN` to avoid a
company judgment. `INVALID_FOR_ATTRIBUTION` for the experiment: good prose and
correct caution do not repair a contaminated Baseline.
