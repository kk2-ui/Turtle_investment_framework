# CN:000726 Outcome Source-Clock Mismatch

The official CNINFO annual-report classification returned no full FY2019
annual report published by the frozen due date `2020-05-01`. Only the FY2018
annual report appeared in the authorized query window. No later report body,
target outcome value, price or return was read.

## What remains valid

- The target was prospectively selected.
- Baseline-before-learning and Enhanced-after-learning were frozen before any
  target outcome metadata access.
- Independent review accepted that the learning materially changed the
  research action and capital-allocation credit threshold.
- `TRANSFER_CANDIDATE_CREATED_PREOUTCOME` remains a valid result-before-access
  status.

## What is not earned

- No target enterprise judgment is settled.
- No Baseline/Enhanced decision-utility comparison is possible.
- `TRANSFER_VALIDATED` remains denied.

## Next action

This is the second development episode in which a one-year source clock ended
before a usable FY2019 report. The response is not a new company-wide gate and
not a wider retrospective search. The next target uses an earlier normal
reporting cycle (`2018-05-01` cutoff, FY2018 outcome due `2019-05-01`) and is
selected on pre-outcome business materiality before its result metadata is
queried. A source-clock mismatch will remain local if it recurs.

The investor judgment does not become “cannot judge”: CN:000726 remains a
strong operating company with observed build execution and unproven economic
absorption. What remains unknown is whether the frozen FY2019 observation
would have changed that treatment.
