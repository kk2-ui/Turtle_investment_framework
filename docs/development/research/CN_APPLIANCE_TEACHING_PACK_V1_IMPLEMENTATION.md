# China Appliance Teaching Pack V1

> Status: `READY_FOR_TEACHING_ONLY / RESULT_ACCESS_NOT_OPENED / NOT_EVALUATION_ELIGIBLE`

## Investor use now

The five companies can now be used to practice the part of judgment that comes
before a forecast: separating an operating mechanism from a convenient story.
Each drill gives a page-bound company state, two live explanations and the
unknowns that still matter.  The learner must map the mechanism, attack it
with the rival explanation and ask for the next evidence.  It cannot declare
a winning company, a causal result, a target price or a buy decision.

| Drill | What the learner practises | What remains deliberately open |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gree C1 | channel and component execution versus industry demand and inventory | owner cash and permanent-loss path |
| Midea C2 | channel simplification and supply coordination versus cycle and acquisition effects | capital return and permanent-loss path |
| Haier C2 | service/logistics delivery system versus product-cycle, global-brand and consolidation effects | domestic owner cash and permanent-loss path |
| Hisense C1 | product commonality, cost work and inventory versus weather/category cycle | capital return, leverage and permanent loss |
| Changhong C2 | white-goods portfolio actions versus multi-product issuer mix | product-boundary cash, leverage and permanent loss |

This is useful training, but not proof that a method predicts better.  The
source packet is historical and a model may remember some later facts, so the
pack is explicitly `MODEL_MEMORY_MITIGATED_NOT_EVALUATION_ELIGIBLE`.  It reads
no result material and creates no score.

## Contract

```text
page-bound J2 episode + mechanism-candidate thread
    -> one teaching drill
    -> existing TEACHING_CASE / WITHIN_CASE_MECHANISM admission
    -> TEACHING_ONLY + RESEARCH_AGENDA
```

The compiler first validates the existing appliance J2/J3/J4 block and source
register.  Each drill must then use exactly one J2 mechanism-candidate thread,
its exact source/page references and all of that episode's `UNKNOWN` or
`EVIDENCE_INELIGIBLE` cells.  It cannot silently substitute a cash-context
thread, drop an unknown, add outcome data or claim a method score.

The compiler delegates permissions to the existing
`TEACHING_CASE / WITHIN_CASE_MECHANISM` claim-specific admission.  No database,
forecast, settlement, peer panel or second training control plane is created.

## Explicit non-rights

- no outcome access, labels or settlement;
- no forecast, probability, score or method transfer;
- no CJO, valuation, report or investment authority;
- no comparative panel or causal verdict.

To graduate any question beyond this pack, a separate Decision Contract,
Outcome Measurement Contract and prefreeze evidence receipt are still required
by J3.  A product-boundary, fixed-peer comparative route remains a separate J4
question.
