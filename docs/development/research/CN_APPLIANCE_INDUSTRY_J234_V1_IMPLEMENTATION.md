## Investor result

The appliance block no longer treats Gree, Midea and Haier as the whole
industry.  It now freezes a five-issuer national risk set across two PIT
cutoffs.  The additional Hisense Kelon and Sichuan Changhong entries prevent a
three-winner story, while remaining deliberately outside any product-level
comparison until their own responsibility-boundary evidence exists.

the three contracts that a later forecast must independently freeze.  J4
states the current honest answer: no homogeneous product-boundary field set is
available, so there is no comparative panel, causal conclusion, method
transfer, CJO, valuation, report or investment authority.

## Why this is a useful industry universe

The economic improvement is not a larger fake peer panel.  It is a wider
starting population from which future research can ask product-specific
questions without survivorship selection.  The core systems make the current
mechanism questions concrete; the two contextual members preserve the fact
that national appliance competition has additional listed issuers and product
boundaries that still need evidence.

The next permitted work is narrow: obtain cutoff-before product, customer,
channel, cash and responsibility-boundary fields for a selected arena.  It is
not permitted to use the present five names as a causal control group or to
read later results.
