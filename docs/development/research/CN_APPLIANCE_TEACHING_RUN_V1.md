## What was trained

This is the first use of the five-company appliance block.  It trains a
researcher to separate an observable operating system from a conclusion about
customer acceptance, cash conversion or competitive superiority.  It is not a
forecast exercise, a scorecard or a post-hoc explanation of which company won.

The run consumes [the closed pack](CN_APPLIANCE_TEACHING_PACK_V1.json), which
in turn is bounded by the [J2/J3/J4 read model](CN_APPLIANCE_INDUSTRY_J234_V1.json)
and its statutory-PDF register.  No outcome file, market price, return,
forecast, settlement, CJO or investment document was opened.

## What the researcher must carry forward

1. **Do not use a visible system as proof of customer acceptance.** Networks,
   logistics, procurement and product upgrades are mechanisms to investigate,
   not a substitute for customer/price/volume evidence.
2. **Keep cash and capital separate.** A reported operating-cash figure does
   not settle maintenance investment, acquisition capital or owner cash.
3. **Make the rival concrete.** Industry demand, raw materials, weather,
   consolidation and multi-business mix are distinct explanations; none should
   be collapsed into a generic “macro” disclaimer.
4. **Ask for the smallest discriminating field.** The next step is a
   product- and responsibility-bound source request, not a broader company
   narrative or a peer ranking.

not establish that any mechanism is true, score a researcher, transfer a
